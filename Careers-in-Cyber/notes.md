# Careers in Cyber

Platform: TryHackMe  
Room: Careers in Cyber  

## Room Overview
This room introduces the variety of careers in cybersecurity and the skills required for each path.

## Key Career Paths Learned
- **SOC Analyst (L1, L2, L3)** – monitors, investigates, and responds to threats  
- **Incident Responder / CERT** – handles cyber emergencies and breaches  
- **Penetration Tester / Ethical Hacker** – tests systems for vulnerabilities  
- **Threat Researcher** – studies malware and hacker tactics  
- **Security Engineer** – builds and maintains security systems  
- **GRC (Governance, Risk, Compliance) Auditor** – ensures policies and regulations are followed  

## Key Takeaways
- Cybersecurity offers many specialized roles  
- Understanding the different career paths helps in choosing a focus area  
- Entry-level roles like SOC Analyst are a common starting point
