# SOC Roles in Blue Team

Platform: TryHackMe
Room: SOC Roles in Blue Team

## Overview
This room explains the different roles inside a Security Operations Center (SOC) and how security teams detect and respond to threats.

## Roles Covered
- SOC Analyst Level 1 – monitors alerts and triages security events
- SOC Analyst Level 2 – investigates alerts in more depth
- SOC Analyst Level 3 / Threat Hunter – proactively searches for threats
- SOC Engineer – builds and maintains security monitoring systems
- Penetration Tester – tests systems for vulnerabilities
- GRC – focuses on governance, risk, and compliance
- Threat Researcher – studies attacker techniques and malware

## Key Takeaway
A SOC team has multiple roles that work together to detect, investigate, and respond to cyber threats.
